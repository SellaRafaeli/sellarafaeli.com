## The 10x Developer Is Real Now

For as long as I've been in this industry, people have argued about the "10x developer". Is there really such a thing as a developer who's ten times as productive as an average one? Or is it a myth that justifies bad behavior from brilliant jerks?

I think the argument just got settled. The 10x developer is real now. It's a senior developer with a frontier AI model.

### What changed this spring

In May, OpenAI released GPT-4o. Last week, Anthropic released Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and I've been using it every day since. For code, it's a real step up: it understands large chunks of a codebase, writes code that usually works the first time, follows instructions precisely, and explains its reasoning clearly. And Anthropic added a feature called Artifacts, where it builds small apps and documents right next to the conversation - you can watch it create a working UI as you describe it.

A year ago, AI coding help felt like a smart autocomplete. Now it feels like pairing with a very fast, very well-read colleague who never gets tired. I'm not exaggerating when I say my working day looks different than it did in January.

### Where the multiplier comes from

I want to be precise about *why* this is 10x and not 1.5x, because it's not just typing speed.

**1. The blank page is gone.** Starting things is the hardest part. Now every task starts from a solid first draft.

**2. Unfamiliar territory is cheap.** A new library, a language I haven't touched in years, an obscure API - I can be productive in an hour instead of a day.

**3. The boring work collapses.** Tests, migrations, boilerplate, documentation, converting data formats. The stuff that used to eat afternoons now takes minutes.

**4. I can explore more options.** Instead of picking one design and committing, I can sketch three, look at them, and choose. Better decisions, not just faster ones.

**5. I can do more of the stack alone.** Things I'd previously hand off - a polished frontend component, a tricky SQL query, a deployment script - I can now do well enough myself, which removes coordination overhead entirely.

Add those up and you get something that does start to look like 10x, at least for some kinds of work.

### Why it's *senior* developers who get the 10x

Here's the part people miss. The AI is equally available to everyone. The multiplier isn't.

When the AI produces code, someone has to know whether it's right. Whether it fits the architecture. Whether it handles the edge case it didn't mention. Whether this is the right thing to build in the first place. A senior developer reads the output, catches the subtle bug, adjusts the design, and moves on in seconds. A junior developer accepts it, ships it, and finds out next month.

**AI multiplies judgment. If you have a lot of judgment, you get a big number.**

That's why I keep talking about [the K-shaped market](/blog/the_k_shaped_developer_market). The tool is the same; the results are wildly different.

### What this means if you hire developers

The economics of software teams are changing in real time:

- **A small team of seniors with great AI tools** can now build what used to require a large team. Fewer people, less coordination, faster decisions.
- **Experience is worth more, not less.** The premium for genuinely senior people should go up, and in my experience it is going up.
- **Give your best people the best tools.** A few hundred dollars a month in AI subscriptions for someone whose time is worth that much per hour is the best deal in software.
- **Measure outcomes, not hours.** A senior developer might ship in three days what used to take three weeks. Paying for hours makes less sense every month.

### Don't get too comfortable

I'm genuinely thrilled about all this - it's the most fun I've had programming in years. But this is moving fast. What's 10x today will be table stakes in two years, and the tools will keep taking on more of the work. The skills that keep you on top are the ones the tools amplify rather than replace: understanding problems, designing simple systems, communicating clearly, and taking responsibility for results.

**The 10x developer was a myth. The 10x developer with an AI is not.**
